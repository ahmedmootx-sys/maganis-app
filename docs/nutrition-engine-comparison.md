### Root Causes of Discrepancies

1. **Target Caloric Surpluses and Deficits**:
   - **`computeEnergyPlan` (Home/Dashboard)** uses a flat percentage of TDEE: `+10%` for gain and `-15%` for cut/fat loss.
   - **`calculateCalorieMacroTargets` (Nutrition / Reports)** uses fixed goal/pace kcal offsets from Helms / ACSM standards: standard gain = +352 kcal, standard loss = -651 kcal (slow: -325, fast: -977).
2. **Protein Multipliers**:
   - **`computeEnergyPlan`** sets protein per kg BW to `2.0 g/kg` for hypertrophy/recomp, `2.2 g/kg` for cut, `1.8 g/kg` baseline (Morton et al. 2018).
   - **`calculateCalorieMacroTargets`** sets protein to `1.8 g/kg` baseline for maintenance/gain, and up to `2.14 g/kg` for deficit.
